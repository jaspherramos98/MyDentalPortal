1. Who we are

This system is operated by [Operator / Company name] on behalf of [Clinic name], which is the Personal Information Controller of the patient records stored here. The system operator acts as a Personal Information Processor for the clinic.

2. Data we collect
  • Personal information: name, birthdate, sex, address, contact details, occupation, emergency contact, guardian (for minors).
  • Sensitive personal / health information: medical and dental history, conditions, allergies, medications, vital signs, treatment records, dental charts, prescriptions, and uploaded photos/documents.
  • Account information (clinic staff): name, email, professional license number, role.
3. Why we process it (lawful basis)

To provide and document dental care, manage appointments and billing, and meet legal/record-keeping obligations. Health information is processed with the patient's consent and as necessary for medical treatment, consistent with Section 13 of the Data Privacy Act.

4. How we protect it

Access is restricted to authorised, authenticated clinic accounts on a need-to-know basis. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest, access is logged, and the system applies role-based access control and other technical safeguards.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. It is shared only with the treating clinic's authorised personnel, service providers strictly necessary to run the system (e.g. hosting, database), and where required by law. Service providers are bound by confidentiality and data-processing terms.

6. Retention

Records are kept only as long as necessary for the purposes above and for the period required by applicable medical record-keeping rules [specify retention period], after which they are securely disposed of.

7. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you may: be informed; access your data; object to or withdraw consent for processing; correct inaccurate data; have data erased or blocked; obtain a copy of your data (portability); and be indemnified for damages. To exercise these rights, contact the Data Protection Officer below.
